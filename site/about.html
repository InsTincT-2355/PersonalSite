About me

Hi, I'm Ahmed.

Builder, business owner and lifelong learner from Muscat, Oman.

I'm Ahmed Al Touqi, 25, from Muscat. I fell in love with technology the day I built my first PC: picking every part, wiring it together, and watching it boot for the first time. That curiosity about how things work has stayed with me ever since.

Away from the screen, you'll usually find me with a book on mythology. I love the old stories of gods and heroes, and what they say about people.

Where I'm heading is simple: I want to become an AI engineer, building intelligent systems that make real work easier.
